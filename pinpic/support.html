Support

Need help with Pic Pin? Email SayWhat.IyzPink@gmail.com, or open Help in the app and choose Send Feedback.

Include your device model, iOS version, Pic Pin version and the steps that led to the problem. Screenshots are optional; remove private information before sending them. No response time is guaranteed.

Keeping media handy

Choose or capture a photo or video, optionally name it, then save it to Temp Pins or a folder. Photos support zoom and recognized-text tools; videos support playback. Moving an item to a folder removes it from Temp Pins while keeping it in your library.

Free, trial and Pro

Free holds ten photos or videos total, including items in folders. Every photo or video counts as one. The genuine Pro trial lasts seven days. Pro is a separate one-time purchase, with no subscription or automatic renewal. Apple shows the localized price before you pay.

After trial expiry, locked files remain saved but cannot be viewed, shared, exported or recovered without Pro. Library export requires Pro or an active trial. Purchased Pro has no scheduled expiry.

Purchases and restoration

Purchase, trial activation and Restore Purchases require Apple connectivity. Use the Apple Account that owns the purchase. Already verified ownership can work offline; a fresh installation may need an online restore. Restore Purchases does not restore deleted media.

Offline use and permissions

Available app-owned media, names, folders, search and saved recognized text work offline within your access level. Supported iPhone photo recognition runs on-device. Cloud-only Photos assets must download before pinning. Cloud sharing destinations and email may need connectivity.

If capture or import fails, check Pic Pin’s camera, microphone and Photos permissions in iOS Settings. Cancelling a picker does not remove existing pinned items. Live Photos currently import as still images; paired motion is not preserved.

Backup and export

Export My Library creates an unencrypted ZIP of originals and available metadata. Save it somewhere private outside Pic Pin. Backup import into Pic Pin is not currently implemented; exported originals can be opened with ordinary tools. Deleting Pic Pin can remove its local library.

Future updates

Pic Pin Pro is a one-time purchase with no recurring fees. We expect to continue improving Pic Pin for years to come, but future updates, ongoing maintenance, and compatibility with future iOS versions are not guaranteed. These details do not limit applicable consumer rights.
