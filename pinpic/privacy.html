Privacy policy

Updated October 11, 2026.

Pic Pin lets you keep photos and videos handy on your device. This policy explains how the app and these information pages handle data.

What stays in Pic Pin

Pic Pin saves copies of media you select or capture in its app storage, along with names, folders, viewing positions and recognized photo text. It does not automatically upload your library to a Pic Pin server. Your device and operating-system backup settings may create system-managed copies.

Moving an item into a folder keeps it saved. Removing an item deletes Pic Pin’s copy after the available Undo period; it does not delete the original in Photos. Deleting the app can remove its local library.

Permissions and text recognition

Photo access lets you select photos and videos, including downloading cloud-only originals when needed. Camera access lets you capture photos or videos. Microphone access lets you record sound with video.

On iPhone, supported photo text recognition uses Apple’s on-device Vision engine. Photos are not sent to a Pic Pin server for recognition. Videos are not transcribed. Recognized text is stored locally for search and copying.

Purchases and access

Apple handles purchases and restoration. Pic Pin stores purchase and trial history in protected on-device storage to support offline access. Purchased Pro has no scheduled expiry. The genuine trial lasts seven days from its original Apple purchase date. Locked files stay saved after expiry, but cannot be viewed, shared, exported or recovered without Pro. Library export requires Pro or an active trial.

Restore Purchases restores eligible Apple purchases, not deleted media. Protected purchase records may survive an iOS reinstall.

Sharing, exports and external links

When you choose to share, export or open a recognized link, the destination you choose may receive the information and applies its own privacy policy. Library exports are unencrypted ZIP archives containing originals and available library metadata, including names, folders and recognized text. They omit purchase receipts, entitlement records and account or device identifiers. Keep exports private. Importing a backup into Pic Pin is not currently available.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

Pic Pin does not require a user account and does not include advertising, tracking or an analytics SDK in the reviewed app. Apple and your chosen third-party services may process information under their own policies.

Feedback and privacy requests

Send feedback or privacy requests to SayWhat.IyzPink@gmail.com. Pic Pin opens an email draft; it does not send feedback automatically. Information you choose to email is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue. Include only information you intend to share. You may ask about or request deletion of information you sent by email; applicable legal requirements may affect what can be deleted. Your email provider also handles messages under its own policies.

These web pages

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages have no added analytics, advertising scripts or contact form.

Policy changes

Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised date. Contact IyzKold P2S Studios using the address above with questions.
